Privacy Policy for AI Agent

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Introduction

AI Agent is a personal assistant application.

Information You Provide

Messages and voice input may be processed by the app's backend and AI service to generate responses.

Voice Input

The app may use your device's speech recognition feature to convert speech into text.

Data Storage

The backend may store information such as your preferred name to support memory features.

Third-Party Services

AI responses may use Google's Gemini service. Information sent to that service is subject to Google's privacy terms.

Data Security

Please do not submit passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information.

Contact

For questions, contact the developer through the AI Agent GitHub repository.
